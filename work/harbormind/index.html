The problem

Point estimates for port congestion hide risk: knowing an average wait time of 22 hours is dangerous if the realistic window spans 11 to 38 hours during berth disruptions, especially at sea where connectivity is intermittent.

What I built

I processed historical NOAA MarineCadastre AIS vessel trajectories (2023–2025) for Los Angeles / Long Beach and published the resulting cleaned dataset on Kaggle. Alongside that data pipeline, HarborMind pairs LightGBM quantile regression with local offline Gemma 4 agents to explain wait advisories — and explicitly escalates back to the captain whenever prediction spread crosses the safety threshold.
