Does the public source actually support the draft?

Newsletter Draft · v2

The healthy ageing screening programme is available nationwide.

The healthy ageing screening programme is currently being piloted in one district.

Linked Public Source

Inspect the linked source passage to verify the highlighted claim before approval.

“The community screening pilot is limited to one district during phase one.”

The problem

In public health communication, even a subtle overstatement — turning a one-district pilot into a nationwide rollout — erodes trust. Automated summaries without source lineage force editors to re-read everything from scratch.

What I built

I designed a Python pipeline for HealthLab’s supervised newsletter pilot on healthy ageing in Vietnam. It ingests public health articles, preserves original source evidence inside versioned datasets, and prepares source-linked newsletter drafts.

Editors trace individual sentences back to their original sources and bind approval to a specific draft version before export.

How I verified it

Exported evidence datasets can be audited offline, and issue approval locks directly to an exact draft version so no unreviewed edits slip into publication.
